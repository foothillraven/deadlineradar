CPA License Tracking for Your Whole Firm

Every accounting firm has someone who has to make sure every partner's and staff CPA's license stays current — across however many states they're licensed in. One missed renewal slows down engagements and creates real regulatory risk, and most firms track it today by spreadsheet. A spreadsheet fails in three specific ways.

Where a spreadsheet (and an individual CPA's own inbox) falls short

Multi-state blind spot. A state board only reminds a CPA about the license held with that board — nobody sends a nudge about the other one or two states the same person might also be licensed in. Nothing is watching the full multi-state picture except the CPA themselves, one inbox at a time.

No firm-level visibility. The partner or admin who actually carries the regulatory risk for the firm never sees any of this — only the individual licensee's own inbox gets the reminder. If that person doesn't forward it, changes their email, or leaves the firm, the firm has zero visibility until a renewal is already missed.

Filing vs. hours. CPE-hour tracking tools (MYCPE, Illumeo, and similar) track whether staff completed their continuing-education hours. That's a different event from whether the actual renewal filing with the state board happened. Finishing every CPE hour and still missing the filing deadline is a real, common failure mode — this product is about the filing, not the hours.

What you get

A firm-wide view that answers what a spreadsheet can't: who's current, who's at risk, and who needs to act before a deadline — for every staff CPA and the firm's own registration, sourced to the same codified statute or rule we verify for every free state page on this site — see exactly how we verify every deadline. Any individual CPA can already get free reminders on their own; what a firm gets here is the roster-level accountability view nobody's personal inbox provides, in one place.
